About

New kinds of brain maps are driving a renaissance of visual neuroscience, starting with the fruit fly Drosophila. The fly connectome has yielded the first wiring diagram for an entire visual system, and transcriptomics is identifying the ion channels and synaptic receptors of fly visual neurons. With such detailed information, it is now possible to model the fly visual system with unprecedented realism.

During the 2026–27 academic year, the Simons Center for Systems Biology at the Institute for Advanced Study is hosting a special program on modeling fly vision. The program addresses the perception of motion, color, polarization, form, and objects, as well as complex visually guided behaviors such as navigation and courtship.

See the program announcement at the Institute for Advanced Study for more information.
